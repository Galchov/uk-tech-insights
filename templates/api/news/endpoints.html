News API Endpoints

Method Endpoint Description
Public Read-Only Endpoints
GET /api/news/articles/ List all published news articles (internal + external)
GET /api/news/articles/<slug>/ Retrieve a single article by slug
GET /api/news/articles/?search=AI Search by keyword
GET /api/news/articles/?category=Cybersecurity Filter by category name
GET /api/news/articles/?type=internal Only internal articles
GET /api/news/articles/?type=external Only external articles
GET /api/news/articles/?ordering=-published_at Sort results (e.g. by date or title)
GET /api/news/articles/?page=2 Paginate results (10 per page)
Internal Article Management (Admins Only)
POST /api/news/internal-articles/ Create a new internal article
GET /api/news/internal-articles/<slug>/ Retrieve a single internal article
PUT /api/news/internal-articles/<slug>/ Replace entire internal article
PATCH /api/news/internal-articles/<slug>/ Partially update internal article
DELETE /api/news/internal-articles/<slug>/ Delete internal article
